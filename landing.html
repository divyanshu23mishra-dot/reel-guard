How it works

  1. 1

    Set your limit

    Pick how many Reels you want to allow yourself each day.

  2. 2

    Scroll normally

    Open Instagram and use Reels the way you always have.

  3. 3

    ScrollFit counts

    Each new Reel you watch is detected and added to today's total.

  4. 4

    Reach your limit

    A calm full-screen reminder appears — no shame, just a stop.

  5. 5

    Track your progress

    See streaks, scores, and trends build up on your dashboard.

Why ScrollFit?

Short-form content is built for endless scrolling — there's no natural stopping point. ScrollFit gives it one, and turns the habit into something you can actually measure and improve, the same way you would a workout.
